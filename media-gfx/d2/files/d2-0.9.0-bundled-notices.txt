D2 0.9.0 supplemental bundled notices
====================================

D2 source: https://github.com/d2lang/d2
Version: v0.9.0
Source commit: d5a51743b6d5c00f1ec6f8340003f5d5a6ba4eda
Upstream notice retrieval date: 2026-10-23

This file supplements, and does not replace, D2's LICENSE.txt,
THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt, package-local notices, and the license and notice
files supplied by its Go modules. The companion d2-0.9.0-module-sources.txt
identifies module sources and the source-availability statement for elk-go.
The section introductions describe provenance; the reproduced legal notices
retain their original attribution and terms. Source comment markers have
been removed from the inline notices. License text may be line-wrapped.
